How to use Twin Player Choose your local videos, then press Play. Enable Single video to watch only the first video. Use the timeline to seek. The right timer shows remaining recording time. Space · Play or pause ← / → · Skip 10 seconds Option + ← / → · Change speed Shift + ← / → · Maximize the corresponding panel Audio selects the sound source. Panel sizes adjusts the split. In fullscreen, move the mouse to reveal controls. Escape exits fullscreen. Space and arrow seeking work when a form control is not focused.
